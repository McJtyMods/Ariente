1.20-0.0.31-alpha:
- Added the Ore Purifier: feed Silk Touch ore blocks into a Purifier Start and recover two to five raw metals at a Purifier Finish. Automatically supports tagged vanilla and modded ores with raw-material forms. One to four transparent tubes increase recovery, time and flux costs, with floating ores, a gradual raw-metal transformation, and rotating red particle rings. Includes holographic inventories, item automation, blueprints and JEI recipes. Each tube uses 400 of each flux per tick; four tubes need two generator tanks of each type for full speed, with slower operation on smaller supplies.
- Lithium, Manganese, Platinum and Silver ores now drop raw metals with Fortune and Silk Touch support. Smelt or blast raw metals into ingots; ore blocks remain smeltable. Added shared Forge raw-material and ore-category tags.
- Energy Lance and ship lasers travel 20% faster. Ships flash red when hull hits reach their pilots, and abandoned ships descend four times faster. The ship cannon now defaults to Space, which no longer adds upward thrust.
- Strengthened powered Armor Upgrades with armor toughness and laser protection. Enemy scout cannons retain their rapid bursts with lower damage per shot, and Forcefield now works alongside Armor and Regeneration with Energy Efficiency.
- Added the operator-only /ariente equip command for upgraded power armor, an Enhanced Energy Sabre, Energy Lance, a heavily fueled Energy Holder and a fully charged Flux Ship. The armor's forcefield starts disabled.
- Newly crafted and creative-inventory Flux Ships start empty and show "No power" in the cockpit. Recovered ships retain their charge.
- Flux Ship tooltips now explain deployment and charging, with clear feedback when deployment is blocked.
- Added Flux Ship batteries and a Charger with a rotating, glowing top powered by both flux types. Charging takes about one minute; full-speed endurance is about twenty minutes, with much lower hovering and descent costs. The cockpit now shows charge, and empty ships gently descend.
- Flux Ship exhaust is longer and brighter, with clearer distant strands. Enemy ships bank more steeply and pass half of incoming laser damage to their pilots while their hulls remain indestructible.
- Flux Ships now steer toward mouse look, including climbs and dives, with A/D turning assistance and Left Alt free look. W/S retain smooth movement and braking. Mouse steering can be disabled in the client configuration.
- Flux Ships can now carry soldier pilots, with smooth climbing and diving turns, stable flybys and a seated model that follows the hull. Defeating an enemy pilot disables its ship.
- Flux Ships now have a front-mounted rapid-fire laser with Energy Lance damage. Hold Space (rebindable) to fire and aim with the hull, using the new cockpit crosshair and compact flight instruments.
- Added Fluid and Forge Energy display modules with Constructor blueprints, configurable numbers, meters and histories, fluid tank selection and units, and FE capacity, percentage and net buffer change readings.
- Flux Ships now have a flight engine hum based on the Flux Levitator sound, with smooth volume changes and a gentle pitch rise as speed increases. Pilots hear a cockpit loop; nearby observers hear positional sound.
- Flux Ships have flowing yellow negarite and cyan posirite exhaust strands from the rear engine bay. The glow grows with flight speed and is visible in third person and on other ships.
- Flux Ships now have a transparent mounted cockpit hologram with speed, altitude and current controls. Flight reaches about 374 km/h, with smooth acceleration, braking and reversals, banking, climb/descent pitch and adjustable first-person camera tilt. Unattended ships gently sink to the ground. In third person, the pilot sits facing forward and tilts with the hull while the camera can look around.
- Added Flux Slope Beams for one-block inclines. Levitators, passengers and both control panels follow climbs and descents, with automatic speed limiting around slopes.
- Flux Levitators now use your forward/back movement keys (W/S by default) to accelerate in the direction you face or brake gradually. Release the keys to keep your speed; the hologram's red button still stops instantly.
- Fixed Flux Levitators failing to restart when stopped on a Flux Bend Beam.
- Fixed distant Flux Levitators being audible through cities and underground, with smoother sound when starting and stopping. Interior cart sound no longer plays for other players' carts.
- Added decorative Alien Text Modules using sixteen alien glyphs, with configurable text, size and color. These modules have no crafting recipe or blueprint and can be salvaged from ArienteWorld ruins.
- Holo Storage Cubes now have colored outer frame bands to distinguish their three tiers, with plain blackmarble interiors.
- Output Item Nodes now have compact arrow buttons for batch size. Sensor Item Nodes show labeled Positive and Negative conditions and double arrows to adjust the threshold by 10.
- Automation Fields now cover the interior of a closed Field Marker border, including the bottom layer, leaving room there for nodes and machines. Existing filled marker layouts still work.
- Creative-mode clicks on Smelter and 2x2/3x3 Storage Cube item slots now extract items without breaking the block. Empty slots and held clicks remain protected; a fresh click on the casing still breaks it normally.
- Blueprint Storage, the Constructor and Auto Constructor ingredient panels now show your whole inventory. Unusable items are dimmed and cannot be selected or transferred; hover tooltips still identify them.
- Input Item Nodes can select recipe-aware Smeltable, Wood, Ingots and Other categories in addition to their sample filters. Storage categories exclude smeltable items so a full smelter cannot send unprocessed items to storage.
- Item Node filters now support whitelist and blacklist modes, with clearly labeled Positive and Negative sensor conditions and NBT, Damage and Tags options.
- Item filters, the Constructor, Auto Constructor ingredients and Blueprint Storage now use the Holo Storage player inventory layout, with bordered slots, aligned stack counts and a separated hotbar.
- Reopening a block now moves your existing hologram in front of you, keeping its pin state. Different players retain separate panels.
- Storage holograms now have A-Z and largest-stack-first sorting, a more clearly separated player inventory, and faster scrolling by clicking or holding the side arrows.
- Standardized hologram window controls with compact, bordered close, manual and toggle-pin icons. Pin block panels to keep them open when looking away for the current session. Mounted panels omit close/pin, equipment panels omit pin, and manual buttons appear only with Patchouli and a matching entry.
- Added Holo Storage Cubes with 30, 60 and 120 stack slots, floating item previews, a holographic inventory with hover scrolling and drag or sneak-click transfers, all-sided automation, and increasingly expensive Constructor blueprints.
- Added a 3x3 Storage Cube for nine item types, with the same capacity per type, controls, locking and automation as the original four-type cube. Includes a Constructor blueprint and manual documentation.
- Added the Smelter: furnace recipes at a configurable speed (10 times by default) using 200 of each Ariente flux per active tick by default, two directly accessible front slots with hover highlights, an animated working indicator, sided automation, and a Constructor blueprint. Documented in the guide and Tablet.
- Power armor, Enhanced Energy Sabres and Energy Lances now share a carried Energy Holder for module fuel and charge. Refill through the holder’s own panel or let it collect loose dust automatically. Equipment panels use aligned module columns and show total stored power from all usable fuel and remaining charge, with 1,000 power per dust pair. The holder panel and tooltip show the same total. Removed the redundant Autofeed module.
- Holographic displays now have separate background type, color picker with a color preview button, transparency percentage and border controls. Joined bases share all settings, and existing display presets are preserved.
- Hold right-click to drag installed holographic modules, with green/red placement previews and snapback for invalid drops. Release without dragging to configure.
- GUI question marks now open the relevant Tablet manual entry when Patchouli is installed. Added help to more machine, transport and equipment panels.
- Installed holographic modules can now be configured directly. Modules that outgrow their available space are returned with their edited settings.
- Holographic cycle choices now use recognizable looping-arrow buttons. Numeric settings use sliders with precise arrow controls, including display and elevator heights, lock ranges, shield radius, transport speeds and machine settings.
- Added modular holographic displays: join display bases, choose a surface and height, and install configurable power, storage and colored text modules. Storage modules show bright item previews. All four components have Constructor blueprints.
- Personal forcefields are faintly visible in first person, block lasers and magic, and flash red when ranged hits or explosions trigger a damage-based cooldown. While recharging they absorb 50% of ranged damage and 40% of explosion damage. Melee hits still deal half damage and now reflect the other half as Thorns damage.
- Module tooltips now give a compact effect, compatibility and power usage summary. Updated the guide and Tablet for shared equipment fuel and holographic displays.
- Added the two-handed Energy Lance: a longer sabre-style weapon that fires lasers for 30% more damage than an ordinary sabre, without durability or ammunition. Keep your off hand empty to fire; configure sabre-compatible upgrade modules with the armor configuration key, and craft it with its Constructor blueprint.
- Fixed elevators pulling players back toward the center and holding them below landing edges when trying to walk out.
- Pressing the armor configuration key again now returns to equipment selection, or closes the panel when already on that menu, instead of opening overlapping panels.
- Added a craftable Ariente Tablet with a dedicated technological manual for machines, power, equipment and exploration.
- Ported Ariente to Minecraft 1.20.1 for Forge. Requires HoloGui 1.20-0.0.10-beta or newer.
- Added an ordinary Blueprint Storage crafting recipe so standalone progression can start without another blueprint library.
- Overworld ore distributions can be customized individually with datapacks, and resource ores now also generate in deepslate.

    - Legs upgrades:
        - Speed upgrade
    - Boots upgrades:
        - Step assist

    - Sabre upgrades:
        - Fire aspect
        - Looting

        - Power (extra damage)
    - Global upgrades:
        - Energy: improve energy consumption and max energy usage
        - Autofeed: automatically get negarite/posirite from inventory
